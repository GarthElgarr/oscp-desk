Drop files in this folder, then open Uploads in the desk.
The target pulls them from this app. Do not put anything here you would not serve on the VPN.
To use another folder, start the desk with OSCP_UPLOADS=/home/kali/exam/files
Opening Uploads fetches linpeas.sh and winPEASx64.exe from the PEASS-ng release if they are not already here. They are not committed.
